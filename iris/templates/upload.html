What happens to each document

  1. Pre-processing – resize, denoise, straighten (deskew) and convert to clean black-and-white.
  2. OCR – Tesseract reads the text and reports how confident it is.
  3. Extraction – vendor, GSTIN, invoice number, date, line items, subtotal, tax and total.
  4. Validation – items, subtotal, tax and total are cross-checked; problems are flagged for review.
  5. Categorisation – matched against your category keywords, then saved and added to the dashboard.

Tips: photograph the bill flat, in good light, filling the frame. Thermal receipts fade, so upload them soon.
